From your photo, into light.

This study samples the amber portrait preview into individual GPU-rendered points. Your pointer displaces them; the scatter action sends them outward before they return.

Source shows the static generated portrait used for this experiment. This is an original implementation inspired by the reference, not a copy of its code or its fluid-photo-reveal effect.

Reduced motion is respected. All effects can be paused and the static portrait remains available.
